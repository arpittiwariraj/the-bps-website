Admissions

Join our community and embark on a journey of knowledge and growth.

Admission Process

  1. Inquiry: Fill out the inquiry form below. Your message will be prepared to be sent to our admissions desk via WhatsApp.
  2. Application Form: Obtain the application form from the school office and submit it with the required documents.
  3. Entrance Exam: An entrance exam will be conducted every Friday. The result will be declared after 3 days..
  4. Admission Confirmation: Selected candidates will be notified, and parents will be required to complete the admission formalities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nursery: 3+ years as of 1st April.
  • LKG:4+ years as of 1st April.
  • UKG:5+ years as of 1st April.
  • Class I onwards: Based on performance in the assessment.

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate (copy)
  • Transfer Certificate (original)
  • Previous Year's Report Card (copy)
  • Aadhaar Card of student and parents (copy)
  • 4 Passport-sized photographs
