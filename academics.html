Our Academics

A commitment to academic excellence and holistic development through a modern curriculum.

Our Curriculum

The Base Public School is affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), New Delhi. Our curriculum is designed to be comprehensive, balanced, and child-centered. It aims to develop the cognitive and affective domains of our students.

We focus on an inquiry-based, hands-on learning approach that encourages critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Our academic program is rigorous yet flexible, allowing for the individual needs and talents of our students to be nurtured.

Subjects & Streams

Pre Primary (Nursery-UKG)

Maths,English, Hindi, Art & Craft, Music

Primary (I-V)

English, Mathematics, EVS, Hindi, Computer Science, Art & Craft, Music, Physical Education.

Upper Primary (VI-VIII)

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Social Studies, Computer Science.

Secondary (IX-X)

English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History & Civics, Geography ,Hindi, Physical Education.

Senior Secondary - Science (XI-XII)

Compulsory: English, Physics, Chemistry.
Optional: Mathematics, Biology, Physical Education, Hindi.

Senior Secondary - Commerce (XI-XII)

Compulsory: English, Accountancy, Commerce, Economics.
Optional: Mathematics, Physical Education, Hindi

Extra curriculum

Robotics, Music, Art & Craft, Taekwondo, Dance, Indoor and Outdoor Activities
